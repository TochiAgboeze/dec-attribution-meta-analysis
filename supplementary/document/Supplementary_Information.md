### S10 Fig
![Supplementary Figure S10](figures/supplementary/FigS10_Global_Virulence_Loci_Comparative_Matrix.png)

**S10 Fig.** Global DEC virulome matrix: Diarrhoeal disease association ($\log_2 \text{Odds Ratio}$) across 31 consolidated international cohorts and 29 virulence loci.**
- **Matrix Architecture & Statistical Quantification:** Bioconductor clustered heatmap in R `pheatmap` mapping 31 unique international and African surveillance cohorts against 29 distinct virulence loci. Cohorts with age-stratified reporting (e.g. Garrine et al., Levine et al., Girón et al., Scaletsky et al., Kotloff et al., Ochieng et al.) are consolidated into a single comprehensive row per cohort, with infant- and toddler-specific estimates mapped directly to dedicated columns (`bfpA_infants`, `bfpA_toddlers`, `daaC_infants`, `daaC_toddlers`). Prominently features the landmark Nigerian genomic cohorts of Akinlabi et al. (2023a, 2023b). Cells represent Haldane–Anscombe continuity-corrected effect sizes ($\log_2 \text{Odds Ratio}$ for acute diarrhoea association). Saturated crimson tiles indicate high pathogenic risk ($\log_2 \text{OR} \ge +2.5$, corresponding to $\text{OR} > 5.66$), pure white represents the null association ($\log_2 \text{OR} = 0.0$, $\text{OR} = 1.0$), and cool deep navy tiles denote protective or benign asymptomatic commensal carriage ($\log_2 \text{OR} \le -2.0$). Soft neutral slate tiles (`#F1F5F9`) denote unmeasured determinants. Age annotations are formatted strictly in months (`<12m`, `<24m`, `0–59m`).
- **Functional Column Categorization:** Virulence determinants are organized into 6 molecular functional classes:
  1. *SPATE Autotransporters:* `sepA` (tissue-damaging serine protease), `pet` (plasmid-encoded cytotoxin), `sat` (secreted autotransporter toxin), and `pic` (mucinase / immune evasion protease).
  2. *Fimbrial Adhesins & Receptors:* `aafA` (AAF/II major subunit), `agg3A` (AAF/III), `agg4A` (AAF/IV), `aggA` (AAF/I), composite `AAF_any`, and novel surface adhesin `CS22`.
  3. *Master Regulon & Surface Secretion:* `aggR` (AraC-like master transcriptional activator), `aatA` (dispersin transporter / ABC export system), `aap` (dispersin anti-aggregation protein), `aaiC` (Type VI secretion effector), and `astA` (EAST1 enterotoxin).
  4. *Siderophore Iron Acquisition & Mucosal Fitness:* `irp2` (yersiniabactin biosynthesis) and `fyuA` (ferric yersiniabactin outer membrane receptor).
  5. *DAEC Adhesin Variants & Toxins:* `daaC_all`, `daaC_infants` ($<12\text{m}$), `daaC_toddlers` ($\ge 12\text{--}59\text{m}$), `sat_DAEC`, and `afaE`.
  6. *EPEC LEE Core & Non-LEE Genomic Island OI-122 Effectors:* `eae_unstratified` (intimin), `bfpA_infants` ($<12\text{m}$), `bfpA_toddlers` ($\ge 12\text{--}59\text{m}$), `lifA/efa1` (lymphostatin / mucosal adhesion), `nleB` (GlcNAc-transferase), `nleE` (TAB2/3 methyltransferase), and `astA_aEPEC`.
- **Row Stratification:** Dual hierarchical sidebars delineate the Global Geographic Region (Western Africa, Eastern/Southern Africa, Global Multi-Centre, Latin America, Europe, and Asia & Oceania), Target Age Strata (Infants $<12$m, Toddlers $\ge 12$m, All Paediatric $<5$y), and Primary Pathotype Focus (EAEC, EPEC, DAEC).
- **Core Clinical & Genomic Findings:**
  1. *Universal High-Risk Virulome Drivers:* `sepA`, `aafA`, `CS22`, and genomic island OI-122 effectors (`lifA`, `nleB`) consistently illuminate in vivid crimson across international cohorts, proving that acute diarrhoea is driven by specific tissue-damaging serine proteases and high-affinity fimbriae rather than simple carriage.
  2. *Attenuation of Canonical Markers:* Canonical regulon markers (`aggR`, `aatA`, and `aap`) present predominantly as neutral pale grey or cool blue across multicentre surveillance, graphically explaining why conventional single-locus diagnostics suffer severe positive predictive value failure.
  3. *Ontogenetic Inversion:* Direct visual contrast reveals the age-dependent transition of `bfpA` (strongly pathogenic in infants $<12$m, neutral in older toddlers) and `daaC` (commensal in infants $<12$m, pathogenic in toddlers $\ge 12\text{--}59$m).
