# Supplementary Information

## Supporting Information for:
**Differentiating Clinical Pathogenicity from Asymptomatic Colonization in Diarrhoeagenic *Escherichia coli*: A Systematic Review and Hierarchical Meta-Analysis**

---

### Index of Supplementary Figures

- **S1 Fig. Methodological Quality and Risk of Bias Dashboard (NOS & JBI appraisal).** Itemized methodological quality scoring across 68 African surveillance cohorts and 31 global case-control cohorts evaluated via the Newcastle–Ottawa Scale (NOS) and Joanna Briggs Institute (JBI) critical appraisal checklist.
- **S2 Fig. Continental African DEC Pathotype Prevalence Synthesis.** Multi-panel synthesis displaying pooled random-effects prevalence estimates, Freeman–Tukey double-arcsine distributions, and between-study heterogeneity for EAEC, ETEC, EPEC, STEC, EIEC, and DAEC across primary African surveillance cohorts.
- **S3 Fig. Subregional and Temporal Dynamics of African DEC Pathotypes.** Small-multiples comparative analysis evaluating pathotype shifts across five UN African subregions, longitudinal decadal trends (2000–2026), and the epidemiological transition from typical EPEC (*bfpA*+) to atypical EPEC (*bfpA*-).
- **S4 Fig. Diagnostic Platform Detection Gradients, Head-to-Head Discordance, and Primer Failure Mechanisms.** (A) Empirical DEC detection yield gradients across conventional culture, multiplex PCR, qPCR/TAC, and WGS across 29 primary African cohorts ($N = 19,745$). (B) Head-to-head diagnostic sensitivity and clinical positive predictive value (PPV) against high pathogen load benchmarking conventional PCR vs. quantitative TAC array (Akinlabi et al., 2023a). (C) Molecular root causes of PCR diagnostic discordance and primer failure identified through whole-genome sequencing isolate validation ($N = 185$, Akinlabi et al., 2023b).
- **S5 Fig. Two-Way Hybrid DEC Architecture and Multi-Pathotype Overlap Network.** Circos bilateral mobilome chord diagram and UpSet multi-pathotype intersection matrix quantifying co-occurrence frequencies across 9,360 African children, demonstrating the restriction of hybrids to two-way configurations.
- **S6 Fig. Matched Case-Control Pathotype Disease Attribution Across African Cohorts.** Paired trajectory slope-lollipop plots and random-effects odds ratios resolving true etiological disease attribution from asymptomatic colonization across 13 matched case-control studies.
- **S7 Fig. Global DEC Virulome Meta-Analysis and Locus-Level Disease Attributions.** Forest plots and carrier frequency distributions across 29 discrete virulence determinants spanning EAEC SPATEs and adhesins (*aggR*, *aatA*, *aaiC*, *aap*, *sepA*, *pet*, *pic*, *astA*), EPEC locus of enterocyte effacement and OI-122 effectors (*eae*, *bfpA*, *lifA*, *nleB*, *nleE*), and mucosal fitness/iron-acquisition systems (*fyuA*, *irp2*, *chuA*).
- **S8 Fig. Cross-Continental Virulence Loci Invariance and Comparative Matrix.** Locus-by-locus disease attribution heatmap comparing effect sizes (log2 Odds Ratio) across five continents, confirming invariant clinical pathogenicity profiles independent of geographical origin.
- **S9 Fig. Host Developmental Age-Ontogeny Switch in DAEC and EPEC Susceptibility.** Age-stratified case-control odds ratios demonstrating infant resistance (<12 months) versus toddler vulnerability (12–59 months) for DAEC (*daaC*), contrasted with peak infant vulnerability to typical EPEC (*bfpA*).
- **S10 Fig. Small-Study Effects, Publication Bias Diagnostics, and Funnel Plots.** Funnel plots, Egger's weighted linear regression, Begg's rank test, and trim-and-fill sensitivity models across all 29 virulence loci and 6 pathotype prevalence tiers.

---

### Index of Supplementary Tables

- **S1 Table. PRISMA 2020 27-Item Checklist.** Detailed reporting compliance checklist referencing specific sections and page numbers of the manuscript.
- **S2 Table. Electronic Database Search Strategies.** Comprehensive search strings, boolean operators, and retrieval tallies across PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, EBSCOhost, and AJOL.
- **S3 Table. Methodological Quality and Risk of Bias Evaluation (NOS & JBI Scores).** Itemized quality appraisal and risk of bias scores for all included African surveillance and global case-control cohorts.
- **S4 Table. Primary African DEC Cohorts Included in the Surveillance Synthesis (k=68).** Demographic, clinical, diagnostic, and pathotype recovery metadata across all 68 African surveillance cohorts.
- **S5 Table. Subregional and Diagnostic Modality Pooled Prevalence Breakdown.** Stratified random-effects meta-analyses detailing Freeman–Tukey double-arcsine pooled proportions, 95% confidence intervals, and heterogeneity statistics.
- **S6 Table. Univariate and Multivariate Meta-Regression Models of Between-Study Heterogeneity.** REML variance partitioning models evaluating the influence of publication era, diagnostic modality, and host age strata.
- **S7 Table. Virulome Synthesis Virulence Locus 2×2 Contingency Tables (k=30 cohorts, 154 strata).** Complete primary study-level contingency counts for cases and controls across all 29 discrete virulence determinants.
- **S8 Table. Subgroup and Sensitivity Analyses of Virulence Loci.** Leave-one-out sensitivity models, diagnostic platform stratifications, and publication bias diagnostics across all evaluated loci.
- **S9 Table. Comparative Methodological Framework Benchmarking Against Prior Literature.** Five-dimension architectural comparison benchmarking the present study against prior regional enteric syntheses.
