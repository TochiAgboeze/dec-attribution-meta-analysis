# Supplementary Information

## Supporting Information for:
**Differentiating Clinical Pathogenicity from Asymptomatic Colonization in Diarrhoeagenic *Escherichia coli*: A Systematic Review and Hierarchical Meta-Analysis**

---

### Index of Supplementary Figures

- **S1 Fig. Risk of Bias Dashboard (NOS & JBI appraisal).** Methodological quality appraisal across primary African surveillance cohorts and global case-control virulome studies based on Newcastle–Ottawa Scale (NOS) and Joanna Briggs Institute (JBI) appraisal criteria.
- **S2 Fig. African EAEC Prevalence Forest Plot & Subregional Raincloud Distributions.** Pooled random-effects meta-analysis and subregional prevalence distributions of Enteroaggregative *Escherichia coli* across 26 African paediatric cohorts ($k = 26$, $N = 9,264$).
- **S3 Fig. DEC Pathotype Prevalence Distributions Across Age Strata.** Small-multiples faceted distributions across paediatric age strata (<12 months, 12–59 months, and $\ge 60$ months) for major DEC pathotypes.
- **S4 Fig. African ETEC and EPEC Prevalence Forest Plots.** Study-level random-effects forest plots and pooled prevalence estimates for Enterotoxigenic *E. coli* and Enteropathogenic *E. coli* across African surveillance cohorts ($k = 26$).
- **S5 Fig. Typical vs Atypical EPEC Epidemiological Transition.** Paired cohort comparisons, lineage proportions, and temporal dynamics showing the continent-wide transition from typical EPEC (*bfpA*+) to atypical EPEC (*bfpA*-).
- **S6 Fig. African STEC, EIEC, and DAEC Prevalence Forest Plots.** Pooled random-effects prevalence estimates and between-study heterogeneity for Shiga toxin-producing *E. coli*, Enteroinvasive *E. coli*, and Diffusely adherent *E. coli*.
- **S7 Fig. Subregional and Temporal Era Dynamics of African DEC Pathotypes.** Comprehensive comparative landscape across 5 UN African subregions, 4 surveillance eras (2000–2026), and publication bias funnel plot diagnostics.
- **S8 Fig. Diagnostic Modality Ladder and Detection Disparity Across African Surveillance.** Evaluation of empirical diagnostic yield escalation across culture, biochemical phenotyping, conventional PCR, qPCR/TAC, and WGS.
- **S9 Fig. Diagnostic Platform Yield Boxplots (Endpoint PCR vs qPCR vs WGS).** Empirical detection yield distributions across molecular platforms stratified by WHO syndromic presentation (acute watery, persistent diarrhoea, asymptomatic).
- **S10 Fig. Two-Way Hybrid Pathotype Architecture and Co-Occurrence.** Empirical isolate frequencies, bilateral conjugative plasmid-phage combinations, and co-occurrence chord diagrams across African children ($N = 502$ hybrid isolates).
- **S11 Fig. Multi-Pathotype Genomic Overlap and UpSet Matrix.** Intersecting set visualization and co-infection architecture across 9,360 African children demonstrating absence of three-way or four-way hybrid lineages.
- **S12 Fig. Diagnostic Platform Sensitivity Gradients and Primer-Binding Site Discordance.** Root-cause genetic dissection of false-negative conventional endpoint PCR due to primer-binding site SNPs and plasmid loss.
- **S13 Fig. Matched Case-Control Pathotype Odds Ratios Across 13 African Cohorts.** Paired slope-lollipop trajectory plots and random-effects odds ratios resolving true etiological disease attribution from asymptomatic colonization.
- **S14 Fig. Global Virulome Loci Prevalence Distributions in Cases vs Controls.** Small-multiples carrier frequency boxplots comparing empirical carriage of 29 virulence loci between diarrhoeal cases and asymptomatic community controls.
- **S15 Fig. Cross-Continental Virulence Loci Comparative Heatmap (Africa vs Global).** Comparative virulome association matrix ($\log_2 	ext{Odds Ratio}$) demonstrating cross-continental conservation of locus-level pathogenicity across 5 continents.
- **S16 Fig. EAEC Locus-Level Random-Effects Forest Plots (17 loci).** Multi-panel forest plots displaying pooled odds ratios, 95% confidence intervals, and zero heterogeneity ($I^2 = 0.0\%$) across 17 EAEC virulence determinants.
- **S17 Fig. Host Developmental Age-Ontogeny Switch in DAEC and EPEC Susceptibility.** Age-stratified case-control odds ratios demonstrating infant resistance (<12m) vs. toddler vulnerability (≥12–59m) for DAEC (*daaC*), and infant susceptibility to typical EPEC (*bfpA*).
- **S18 Fig. EPEC OI-122 Effector Island Locus Attribution (lifA, nleB, nleE) Forest Plots.** Random-effects meta-analysis quantifying the substantial disease odds imparted by OI-122 pathogenicity island effectors in atypical EPEC.
- **S19 Fig. Mucosal Iron-Acquisition and Fitness Loci Attribution Meta-Analysis (fyuA, irp2, chuA).** Random-effects forest plots demonstrating balanced carriage of yersiniabactin and heme receptor genes across diarrhoeal and healthy cohorts.
- **S20 Fig. Master Diagnostic and Characterization Methodology Stack Across 68 African Cohorts.** Master chronological matrix detailing diagnostic technology adoption across all 68 African diarrhoea surveillance cohorts (2000–2026).

### Index of Supplementary Tables

- **S1 Table. PRISMA 2020 27-Item Checklist.** Detailed compliance checklist with specific manuscript section and line references.
- **S2 Table. Electronic Database Search Strategies.** Complete search syntax, boolean operators, and hit counts across PubMed/MEDLINE, Scopus, Web of Science, Embase, EBSCOhost, and AJOL.
- **S3 Table. Methodological Quality and Risk of Bias Evaluation (NOS & JBI Scores).** Itemized scoring across Selection, Comparability, and Exposure domains using Newcastle–Ottawa Scale (NOS) and JBI prevalence tool.
- **S4 Table. Primary African DEC Cohorts Included in the Surveillance Synthesis (k=68).** Granular cohort characteristics, clinical presentation, country, UN subregion, diagnostic platform, sample size, and pathotype recovery rates across surveillance cohorts.
- **S5 Table. Subregional and Diagnostic Modality Pooled Prevalence Breakdown.** Granular subgroup meta-analysis tables with Freeman–Tukey double-arcsine proportions and 95% confidence intervals.
- **S6 Table. Univariate and Multivariate Meta-Regression Models of Between-Study Heterogeneity.** Restricted maximum likelihood (REML) models partitioning between-study variance across publication era, diagnostic platform, and clinical setting ($k = 26$).
- **S7 Table. Virulome Synthesis Virulence Locus 2×2 Contingency Tables (k=30 cohorts, 154 strata).** Complete study-level contingency counts (cases positive/negative vs. controls positive/negative) across 30 cohorts and 154 locus strata.
- **S8 Table. Subgroup and Sensitivity Analyses of Virulence Loci.** Stratified meta-analyses and leave-one-out sensitivity diagnostics across diagnostic platforms and geographical continents.
- **S9 Table. Comparative Methodological Framework Benchmarking Against Prior Literature.** Direct 5-dimension architectural comparison benchmarking the present study against prior African enteric syntheses.
